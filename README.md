# Plan Sketch

A floor-plan sketchpad made for drawing fast on a phone.

- A separate project per job, each with its own plot size
- Walls drawn at the thickness they really are: 4½", 6" or 9"
- Lines locked to 90° while drawing, with corners to line up on
- Erase stops at the lines that cross; delete takes the whole line
- Doors and windows placed on a wall by size and position
- Reference lines, colours, locking
- Sizes typed and shown in feet and inches, always on a half inch

**Open it: https://appujothi.github.io/plan-sketch/**

Your drawings stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere and there is
no account to make. Add the page to your phone's home screen to use it offline.

The whole app is one file, `index.html`.
